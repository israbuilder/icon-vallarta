Designed with the spirit of a world-class luxury resort, **Icon Vallarta** surrounds residents and guests with expansive recreational and wellness amenities curated by Philippe Starck.

## The Icon Beach Club & Pool Deck

- **The Grand Ocean Infinity Pool:** One of the largest beachfront residential infinity pools in Mexico, featuring in-water sun loungers and sweeping views of the Pacific horizon.
- **Beach Club Dining & Bar:** Enjoy poolside lunch, grilled fresh catch of the day, tropical fruit smoothies, and handcrafted margaritas served directly to your sun lounger.
- **Direct Beach Access:** Private gated access leading onto wide golden sands, equipped with umbrellas and attentive beach attendants.
- **Hot Tubs & Outdoor Jacuzzis:** Relax under the stars with therapeutic outdoor hot tubs positioned along the garden promenade.

## Comprehensive Wellness & Leisure Facilities

- **Oceanfront Fitness Pavilion:** State-of-the-art cardio and strength equipment positioned overlooking the swimming pools and palm gardens.
- **Full-Service Spa:** Steam rooms, Finnish saunas, cold plunge baths, and private treatment suites for body wraps, facials, and massages.
- **Private Screening Theater:** 20-seat private cinema with acoustic engineering and 4K projection for private screenings.
- **Children's Play Lounge & Game Room:** Air-conditioned recreation spaces with billiards, games, and secure family entertainment.
